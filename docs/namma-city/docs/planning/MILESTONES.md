# Milestones and Definition of Done

## M0 — Engine Ready
Packaged blank project runs on Ubuntu.

## M1 — Player Ready
Movement and interaction feel responsive in a greybox.

## M2 — Driving Ready
Car, motorcycle, and auto-rickshaw have distinct handling.

## M3 — District Ready
A complete streamed district can be traversed without obvious world gaps.

## M4 — Living City
Traffic and pedestrians populate the district and recover from local navigation problems.

## M5 — Action Loop
Combat, damage, police response, chase, and escape work end to end.

## M6 — Mission Loop
Three complete missions can be played from fresh save to completion.

## M7 — Vertical Slice
Presentation, audio, weather, save/load, and performance are demo quality.

## M8 — Expansion Approved
Only begin city-scale expansion if M7 playtests show the core loop is genuinely enjoyable.
