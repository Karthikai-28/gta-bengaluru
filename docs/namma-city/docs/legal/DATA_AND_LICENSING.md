# Data, Licensing, and Originality

## Core rule

Build an original game. “GTA-like” should describe the genre and broad open-world interaction model, not copied content.

## Do not copy

- GTA characters
- Storylines
- Mission scripts
- Logos
- UI layouts
- Maps
- Audio
- Dialogue
- Vehicle designs unique to GTA
- Weapon names/fictional brands unique to GTA

## Mapping data

Use datasets whose licenses explicitly permit your intended processing and distribution.

OpenStreetMap is a strong candidate for topology, but you must comply with ODbL attribution and database obligations.

## Google Maps / Street View

Treat Google imagery primarily as a reference unless your intended use is explicitly authorized by the relevant Google Maps Platform terms/API. Do not build a production pipeline around scraping, bulk downloading, caching, or extracting textures/geometry from Street View.

## Real brands and landmarks

- Fictionalize commercial brands by default.
- Avoid implying criminal conduct by real companies or people.
- Review landmark, logo, signage, and publicity-right issues before commercial release.
- Keep a `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` file in the project root.
