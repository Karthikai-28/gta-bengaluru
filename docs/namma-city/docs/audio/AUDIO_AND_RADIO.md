# Audio and Radio

## Ambient layers

- Traffic bed
- Horn variations
- Motorcycles
- Autos
- Bus brakes
- Metro ambience where appropriate
- Vendors
- Birds
- Rain
- Construction
- Generator/AC ambience
- Nightlife exterior bleed

## Radio station concepts

- Kannada contemporary
- Indie
- Electronic
- Hip-hop
- Rock
- Classical/fusion
- Talk radio
- Fictional news
- Traffic station
- Satirical startup/business podcast

Use licensed or commissioned music. Do not ship copyrighted commercial tracks without rights.

## Reactive news

News reports can reference major mission outcomes and city-wide incidents to make the world feel persistent.
