# Story Bible

## Working title

**Namma City**

## Genre

Open-world action thriller with satire, urban drama, technology, crime, friendship, and choices.

## Setting

A fictionalized Bengaluru-inspired metropolis. Real neighborhoods can inspire geography and culture, while businesses, political actors, police organizations, and criminal groups should generally be fictionalized.

## Protagonist premise

A Bengaluru native in their late twenties returns after several years away. Their family runs a small vehicle workshop. An old friend disappears while investigating a technology company connected to city infrastructure.

## Central corporation

**AstraGrid Systems** — placeholder fictional company.

Public role:
- Traffic systems
- Smart-city sensors
- Parking systems
- Logistics
- Security platforms

Hidden story role:
- Data manipulation
- Corporate espionage
- Coercion
- Land influence
- Criminal partnerships

## Factions

- AstraGrid Systems
- Meridian Estates
- Blackline Security
- Civic Front
- Metro Wolves
- Independent information brokers
- Fictional city police service

## Act structure

### Act I — Homecoming
Garage, friends, small jobs, city tutorial.

### Act II — The City
Central Bengaluru-inspired districts open. Missing-person mystery expands.

### Act III — Silicon Empire
Corporate campuses and data infrastructure become central.

### Act IV — Infrastructure
Industrial zones, warehouses, highways, deeper conspiracy.

### Act V — Namma City
Faction conflict and final player choice determine who controls the information network.

## Tone rules

- Respect Bengaluru rather than making the city itself the joke.
- Satire should target systems, absurd bureaucracy, startup excess, traffic frustration, corporate behavior, and fictional institutions.
- Avoid caricaturing language communities, migrants, class groups, or occupations.
