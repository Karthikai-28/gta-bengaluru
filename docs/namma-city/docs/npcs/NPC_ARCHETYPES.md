# NPC Archetypes and Characteristics

These are gameplay archetypes, not stereotypes. Individual NPC traits should vary within each class.

| Archetype | Typical zones | Behavior traits | Vehicle tendency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software engineer | Tech parks, apartments, cafes | Phone-heavy, commuter schedules | Cab, bike, scooter, car |
| College student | Campuses, cafes, shopping areas | Groups, evening activity | Bus, metro, scooter |
| Auto driver | Transit hubs, markets | High road knowledge, frequent stops | Auto-rickshaw |
| Delivery rider | City-wide | Fast routing, lane filtering, frequent phone checks | Motorcycle/scooter |
| Shopkeeper | Markets | Zone-local schedule | Scooter/foot |
| Street vendor | Markets, office zones | Static or short-route movement | Cart/foot |
| Security guard | Apartments, offices | Observant, may report crime quickly | Foot |
| Construction worker | New developments | Group schedules | Bus/van/foot |
| Office worker | CBD/tech zones | Rush-hour pattern | Metro/cab/car |
| Tourist | Landmark areas | Slow movement, photos, confused routing | Cab/foot |
| Jogger | Parks/residential | Morning/evening schedule | Foot |
| Police officer | Patrol zones | Perception, pursuit, coordination | Police vehicle/foot |
| Bus commuter | Transit corridors | Queue/wait behavior | Bus/foot |
| Nightlife crowd | Indiranagar/Brigade-like zones | Group movement, late schedule | Cab/foot |

## Personality dimensions

Each spawned civilian draws values from distributions:

- Patience
- Curiosity
- Aggression
- Fear
- Lawfulness
- Driving aggressiveness
- Socialness
- Alertness
- Risk tolerance

This prevents every member of an archetype from behaving identically.
