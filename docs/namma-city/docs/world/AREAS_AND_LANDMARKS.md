# Bengaluru Areas, Identity, and Gameplay Use

> Use real places as inspiration. For a commercial game, review trademark, likeness, signage, and location-specific legal considerations. Fictionalize businesses and sensitive organizations.

| Area inspiration | Famous/recognizable for | Game identity | Gameplay ideas |
|---|---|---|---|
| MG Road | Central commercial boulevard, metro, shopping | Downtown spine | Chases, nightlife, police-heavy missions |
| Brigade Road | Retail and nightlife | Neon shopping corridor | Foot pursuits, crowds, rooftop shortcuts |
| Cubbon Park | Green central park | Urban breathing space | Chase escape, stealth, photography |
| Vidhana Soudha vicinity | Government architecture | Monument district | Story set-pieces, restricted areas |
| Commercial Street | Dense shopping streets | Crowd-heavy market | Pickups, pursuit on foot, delivery missions |
| Chickpet | Historic wholesale/market lanes | Tight old-city maze | Motorbike routes, smuggling fiction, foot chases |
| Majestic | Bus/rail transport hub | Chaotic transit core | Multi-level pursuit, crowds, buses |
| Koramangala | Startups, restaurants, nightlife | Young mixed-use district | Early-game home base, social missions |
| HSR Layout | Residential + startups | Cleaner grid district | Street racing, apartment missions |
| Indiranagar | Restaurants, nightlife, residential | Premium nightlife district | Social missions, clubs, car culture |
| Jayanagar | Residential and commercial blocks | Mature neighborhood | Character-driven story missions |
| Electronic City | Large technology campuses and expressway | Corporate tech zone | Infiltration, high-speed chases |
| Whitefield | IT parks, apartments, malls | Modern high-rise zone | Corporate story arc, traffic congestion |
| Peenya | Industrial region | Warehouses and factories | Vehicle jobs, industrial action missions |
| Hebbal | Flyovers and lake vicinity | Infrastructure zone | Vehicle stunts, highway pursuit |
| Silk Board-inspired junction | Famous congestion | Traffic challenge landmark | Signature traffic missions |
| Airport corridor | High-speed roads | Long-distance high-speed zone | Supercar/bike missions |
| Nandi/outskirts-inspired region | Hills and countryside | Escape from city density | Rally routes, hideouts, scenic missions |

## Prototype district recommendation

Compress an area inspired by **MG Road + Brigade Road + Cubbon Park + Commercial Street** into approximately 2 × 2 km. This gives maximum gameplay variety per square kilometer.
