# World Overview

## Design target

Create a **compressed Bengaluru**, not a meter-perfect reconstruction. Preserve identity, relative feel, major corridors, architecture, traffic patterns, landmarks, climate, and neighborhood contrast.

## District philosophy

Each district must have:

- A visual identity
- A traffic identity
- A pedestrian identity
- Day/night differences
- At least one signature gameplay activity
- At least one mission-useful landmark
- Different socioeconomic and architectural character without caricature

## Suggested world regions

1. Central Bengaluru
2. South residential / startup belt
3. East technology corridor
4. West industrial / transport region
5. North highway / airport corridor
6. Outer lakes, quarries, villages, and peri-urban zones

## Compression rules

- Preserve important road connections, not exact road length.
- Compress long commutes aggressively.
- Keep major landmark approach roads recognizable.
- Remove repetitive blocks.
- Increase useful intersections and shortcuts.
- Add alleys, parking lots, service roads, rooftops, and interiors where gameplay benefits.
