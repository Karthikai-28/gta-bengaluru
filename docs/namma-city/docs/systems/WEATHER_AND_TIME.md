# Weather and Time System

## Time-of-day

- Dawn
- Morning commute
- Midday
- Evening commute
- Night
- Late night

Each period changes traffic, pedestrian density, lighting, shop activity, and ambient audio.

## Weather

Vertical slice:

- Clear
- Cloudy
- Rain

Later:

- Heavy monsoon rain
- Mist/fog in outskirts
- Storm events

## Rain gameplay effects

- Wet road material
- Reduced tire grip
- Increased braking distance
- NPC umbrella/shelter behavior
- Reduced pedestrian density in exposed spaces
- Traffic slowdown
- Puddle and spray FX
