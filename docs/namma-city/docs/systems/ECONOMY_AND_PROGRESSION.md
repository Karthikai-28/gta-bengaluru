# Economy and Progression

## Money sinks

- Vehicles
- Vehicle repair
- Vehicle customization
- Clothing
- Safehouses
- Garages
- Businesses
- Mission equipment
- Cosmetic phone customization

## Income sources

- Story missions
- Side jobs
- Races
- Delivery jobs
- Taxi/auto jobs
- Garage work
- Business income
- Exploration challenges

## Progression axes

Avoid simple numeric leveling everywhere. Progress should unlock:

- Contacts
- Areas
- Missions
- Better garages
- Equipment access
- Vehicle classes
- Businesses
- Fast-travel options
- Story choices
