# Weapons and Ammunition

Use fictional names and designs. Avoid copying named GTA weapons or exact branded real-world models.

- Sidearms

- Mission-specific fictional equipment

## HUD format

```text
30 / 240
```

- 30 = loaded magazine
- 240 = reserve ammunition

## Safety/design note

For game documentation, focus on game balance and abstract stats. Do not make the design dependent on real-world weapon construction procedures.
