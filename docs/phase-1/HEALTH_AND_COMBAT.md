# Health, recovery and melee combat

Implemented in source; engine compilation and gameplay verification are pending the workspace-credit approval block. The already-packaged game does not contain these features.

- 100 health and 100 stamina, visible on foot and on the cycle. Health persists through get-up; explicit respawn restores it.
- Left mouse: attack. One key; the situation picks the strike, in the manner of GTA's melee (`NammaCombatBrain.h`). In punching range successive presses walk jab, cross, jab and a finisher (a roundhouse kick with room to swing, otherwise a cross); the chain continues while the next press comes within 0.6 s of the last strike ending, and the guard is held in between. When only a leg reaches, the first press is a front (push) kick and later ones roundhouse kicks. A guarding opponent is shoved off with a push kick; a downed one is kicked. Beyond kicking reach the press throws a step-in jab; with no target at all it shadow-boxes the chain. Punches cost 12 stamina and kicks 22.
- The strikes themselves are motion templates measured from public boxing and kickboxing tutorials (`Art/Motion/README.md`): MediaPipe tracks the coach's skeleton, `Scripts/extract_strike_motion.py` finds and labels each demonstration, and the median trajectories of the fist, elbow, foot, knee, pelvis and torso become `NammaStrikeData.h`. Four strikes ship: jab, cross, front kick and roundhouse kick. Hooks and uppercuts are deliberately absent: the tutorials available yielded no demonstration the extractor could tell from a coach holding an arm out or raising the guard, so rather than invent them they wait for better footage. In game the fist or foot is placed relative to its shoulder or hip in limb lengths and solved with two-bone IK using the tracked elbow or knee as the pole, so the elbow goes where the boxer's did. The previous hand-tuned elbow poles had their sign wrong for Manny's component space (the character's left is +X) and pulled every elbow across the chest. The one hit check is at each template's measured impact time; durations are the quicker third of the demonstrations, clamped to 0.3–0.6 s for punches and 0.6–1.0 s for kicks.
- Right mouse: hold guard. Hits within the forward guard cone lose 80% of their damage if sufficient stamina is available. A guard spends 0.8 stamina per incoming damage point; exhausted guards fail. Stamina regenerates at 22 per second outside attacks, guard, recovery and ragdoll.
- Damage: jab 10, cross 16, front kick 22, roundhouse kick 28; kicks add 40% extra stagger and knock back harder. Rapid blows accumulate stagger; enough stagger causes a nonfatal knockdown. The hit sweep runs from the striking limb's root to where the template lands it (fist-sized for punches, foot-sized for kicks) and stops at walls and the first blocking target. There are no weapons or ranged attacks in this version.
- Normal falls apply game damage above 7 m/s; cycle ejections use a lower 3 m/s threshold. Both use `min(100, 3 * (speed - threshold)^2)` above that threshold. Ordinary jumps do not damage health. This is gameplay tuning, not an injury simulation.
- After a nonfatal ragdoll, X requests getting up locally. Automatic recovery starts after 1.5 seconds, provided the body has settled and a standing capsule fits. The character rises through a 0.8 second procedural crouch/leg-IK pose. This is not a motion-captured prone/supine get-up animation. Blocked standing space leaves the character down instead of teleporting through a wall.
- Once recovered, approach the same cycle and press E to ride again. Recovery does not heal or reset the delivery.
- At zero health, the character remains down. R respawns. The pause-menu delivery restart also resets the sparring partner.
- A passive sparring partner spawns about 9.5 metres ahead of PlayerStart on clear ground. They fight by the same decision function as the player, southpaw (the templates mirrored), retaliate when hit, kick from range every third strike of a chain, stop engaging downed characters and riders, and have a limited pursuit radius. Movement uses collision-swept direct pursuit; it does not yet navigate around complex obstacles.

Host tests cover health bounds, stamina, blocking/exhaustion, lethal damage, stagger and impact thresholds; the strike templates (durations, impact inside the strike, return to guard, mirroring, and that no reference elbow ever crosses inward past its shoulder); and the strike decision table. Native Unreal tests cover the first close-range strike being a jab whose left elbow stays outside the line to the fist, cooldown, a front kick at kicking range that lifts the foot while the support foot stays planted and damages the opponent, get-up location, death/respawn and local recovery/remount.
