# Vehicle Branding, Design and Data Policy

Real vehicle names in this documentation are **reference labels only**. For a commercial game:

1. Do not ship third-party trademarks, model names, logos, liveries or distinctive protected artwork unless licensed.
2. Prefer an original fictional manufacturer ecosystem with recognizable vehicle classes rather than one-to-one copies.
3. Record the source and license of every photo, CAD file, sound recording and measured dataset.
4. Do not scrape or redistribute proprietary configurator/CAD assets.
5. Verify factual technical specifications against authoritative sources for the exact model year/variant before publishing them as facts.
6. Simulation parameters may deliberately differ from real values; label them as game tuning data.
7. Police, transport-company, municipal and corporate liveries should be fictionalized unless permissions are obtained.
